Logging and Monitoring Policy

All production services must emit structured logs (JSON) with request_id, user_id, and error_code.

Logs must be retained for 30 days in hot storage and 365 days in archive storage.

PII must be redacted before writing logs.

Critical alerts:
- p95 latency > 300ms
- error rate > 2%
- authentication failures > 1% of requests
