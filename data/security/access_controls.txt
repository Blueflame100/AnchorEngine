Security Access Control Standards

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all production systems and administrative accounts.

Principle of least privilege: Users should only have access to resources necessary for their role.

Access reviews must be conducted quarterly for all privileged accounts and annually for standard accounts.

Failed authentication attempts:
- 3 failures: Account locked for 15 minutes
- 5 failures: Account locked for 1 hour
- 10 failures: Account requires manual unlock by administrator

All access changes must be logged and auditable. Privileged access sessions must be recorded and reviewed monthly.
