Security Incident Response Procedures

All security incidents must be reported within 1 hour of detection to the Security Operations Center (SOC).

Severity levels:
- Critical: Active data breach or system compromise
- High: Unauthorized access detected
- Medium: Suspicious activity requiring investigation
- Low: Policy violations or minor anomalies

Response timeline:
- Critical: Immediate containment within 15 minutes
- High: Containment within 1 hour
- Medium: Investigation within 4 hours
- Low: Review within 24 hours

Post-incident review must be completed within 7 days and include root cause analysis and remediation steps.
